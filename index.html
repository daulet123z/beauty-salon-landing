Our story

A studio built around you

Velvet & Bloom began in 2018, when stylist Elena Ruiz turned a sunny former flower shop on Linden Avenue into four chairs, two nail stations and one very good kettle.

We keep our books small on purpose. Every appointment starts on time, nobody feels rushed, and you leave with advice you can actually use at home.
